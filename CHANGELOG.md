# Changelog

## 1.3.0

Duedial is now on iPhone and iPad.

- **iPhone and iPad:** a native app with the same features and reminders scheduled even when the app is closed
- Backups move between Android and iPhone: export on one, import on the other

## 1.2.0

- Renewly is now **Duedial**, with a new dial icon
- Backups made with Renewly import into Duedial

Duedial installs as a new app next to Renewly. To move your data: in Renewly, open Settings → Export, then in Duedial open Settings → Import and pick that file. After that you can uninstall Renewly.

## 1.1.0

- Plan types: subscription, free trial, free (student offers, free tiers) and lifetime
- Free trials: reminder before the trial ends with the last day to cancel, and "I cancelled the trial"
- Free plans: optional end date with a reminder, and the normal price you're saving
- Lifetime purchases: paid once, never renewed or counted monthly
- Cancel a subscription but keep it active until the end of the paid period
- Split the cost between 2 to 6 people; totals use your share
- Regular price, to see what a discount saves
- "Count in monthly bill" switch per subscription; longer plans are divided per month
- Home total is now *Monthly bill* or *Due this month*
- 2-day reminder option
- Backups include the new details; older backups still import
- Existing data is upgraded automatically

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Track subscriptions with name, nickname, fee, currency, category, payment card (nickname and last 4 digits) and notes
- Billing periods: weekly, monthly, every 3 or 6 months, yearly, or custom
- Next payment, last payment and paid so far
- Monthly total with three options: monthly plans only, all per month, or due this month
- Brand colours for about 40 known services, or your own icon from the gallery
- Sort by next payment, price or name; filter by category
- Renewal reminders: on the day, 1, 3 or 7 days before, or off
- JSON backup export and import
- Fully offline, with no internet permission
