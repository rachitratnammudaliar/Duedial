# Privacy

Duedial is built so your data stays with you.

- **No internet.** The app doesn't request the internet permission, so it can't send anything anywhere. The build checks this automatically and fails if it ever changes.
- **No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.**
- **Stored on your phone only.** Subscriptions live in a private database that other apps can't read. Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for Duedial.
- **Card details.** Duedial stores only a name you choose (for example "HDFC Visa") and the last four digits. It never asks for or stores a full card number, expiry date or CVV.
- **Icons.** Images you choose are copied, resized, into the app's private storage. Duedial doesn't keep access to your gallery.
- **Backups** are files you create and keep yourself. They contain your subscriptions in plain text, so store them somewhere private.
- **Notifications** are created on the phone. No server is involved.

Uninstalling Duedial deletes all of its data.
